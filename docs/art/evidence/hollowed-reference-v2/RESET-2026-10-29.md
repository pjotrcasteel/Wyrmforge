## Industry-standard sequence, with review gates

### GATE B0 — sourcing and anatomy
- Start clean from main; retrieve and inspect appropriate **genuine artist sculpt source**, or author a full original organic anatomical sculpt. Verify explicit modification and commercial-game rights. Do not use user-provided image as downloadable geometry.
- A downloaded model is **only a candidate**, never acceptance. Reject unsuitable anatomy *before* rigging, texturing or merging.
- Capture front/side/back **actual Blender** images, and show owner reference alongside them clearly labeled as target versus real candidate.

### GATE B1 — pose and silhouette, before details
- Build full-height edited original **body**, correctly proportioned from real 3D front/side/back views. Include head and hands as **large neutral volumes** for silhouette, without face detail, hair cards, costume, shaders or glow.
- The torso must have believable forward-leaning spine, projecting asymmetric shoulder blades, sharply defined but anatomical chest/waist/pelvis, long gaunt arms with large readable hands/claws and genuinely bent weight-bearing legs.
- Side silhouette MUST distinguish forward displaced neck/head from upright shoulders, pelvis and heels. A more hunched mannequin is still a failure.
- Validate separately: black silhouettes from eight genuine angles, clay geometry from front/side/back, neutral studio framing, 96px game-scale test, stable feet, lack of floating geometry, and original source file reopen.
- Ask for owner steering at **this gate**. Do not disguise a rejected silhouette with materials.

### GATE B2 — connected organic sculpt
Original connected head/neck, shoulders, chest/rib damage, arms, hands, pelvis and knees. Natural anatomical discontinuities and asymmetry, convincing front and back volume.

### GATE B3 — garment silhouette
Only after B1/B2: short leather waist panels, forearm bindings, shin straps. No long coat or cape.

### GATE B4 — face/hair and materials
Only after B1/B2/B3: dead facial anatomy, scalp hair, physical fissures, rough char tissue, bone, leather and restrained recessed ember emission. Avoid orange outlines and fake baked lighting.

### GATE B5 — optimized source, then separate integration approval
Actual native 96px and Ashling scale parity, UV/normal/roughness/emission/source editability and skin deformation tests, reasonable mobile polygon and texture budgets, original game screenshots **after explicitly separate runtime integration authorization**.
