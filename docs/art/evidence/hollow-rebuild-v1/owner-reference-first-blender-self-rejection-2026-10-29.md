## Actual self-review — FAILS owner reference

1. **Head:** visible bald ivory skull cap and synthetic hair curtain; front looks like a clean skeleton or wig. Reference instead has deeply corrupted face, long naturally tangled hair rooted over the entire scalp, violently opened burning mouth, sunken glowing orbits and complex layered tissue.
2. **Torso:** polygonal mannequin shoulders and simple white horizontal rib bars. No believable muscle/skin torn surfaces, asymmetric collapsed shoulder articulation or deep volcanic fissuring.
3. **Back:** a sprinkling of small rigid shards, not connected flowing scorched plates/cracks following spine/scapulae. No physically occluded internal ember material.
4. **Clothing:** straight individual hanging rectangles resemble a crude digital skirt, not the reference's worn wrapped leather layers and irregular frayed cloth. Greaves and boot detailing are still absent.
5. **Hands:** thin natural human fingers with insufficient legible hooked flame-scorched talons.
6. **Framing / native-size:** original helper's steep eight-angle framing clips lower legs on some views; native 96px silhouette remains far below reference quality and needs corrected camera and explicit whole-body bounds checks.
7. **Palette/material:** only simple matte charcoal noise; all critical wound-depth, roughness, bone/skin differentiation and controlled interior emission absent.
