## Disallowed old previews

The previously linked `wet-forest-hunt-demo.mp3`, elemental cast/impact comparisons, and four creature encounter scenes contained **old spell and combat samples**. They are withdrawn and deleted, not listening candidates. Their generating code/workflows are disabled or retired until replacement cues exist.

Only publish demonstrations when every contributing source is reviewed and identified as newly authored/replacement material. Every preview must have an explicit audio source/provenance ledger.

## Review checklist

1. Listen to MP3 on iPhone speaker and headphones.
2. Listen again in game with realistic volume, crowding and repetition.
3. Confirm no old sound bytes were layered, repitched, or remixed into replacements.
4. Verify Ogg SHA256, explicit author/license, sound action identity and mobile playback.
5. Mark accepted/revise/reject; do not merge while placeholders remain.
