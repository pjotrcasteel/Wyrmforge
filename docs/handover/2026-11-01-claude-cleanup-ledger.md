## Exact XYZ pixel and debugging rule from owner

- Requested target: **1m × 1m × 1m physical block**, visible face **32×32 native pixels**, with material and optional lighter/darker faces derived from real C# geology/elevation. **XYZ0 must settle the world-to-camera adapter**; `32px` is not `32m` and provisional `6.4m` in #467 is not acceptance.
- A hypothetical 10cm physical sub-block is **not a literal 3.2-pixel sprite cell**. Keep high-resolution shape/damage truth in C# and derive native 32px observable faces/edge damage and debris deliberately; pixel-cluster design must remain crisp.
- The requested one-source-pixel high-contrast inset around visible block faces is a **debug overlay only**, not an authored material pixel, production feature or texture-layer mask. Its visual contrast can follow dark/light material brightness; turning debug off must leave the original sprite unchanged.
- C# alone owns occupancy, HP, Fireball area damage, nav/LOS/collision, route safety and save/replay; shared G, FXP, animation, VFX and audio consume versioned facts. No second terrain/light/physics engine.
