#### P1 remaining slice: targeting breakable loot-bearing props (recommended; tuning pending playtest)

**Why:** `RunSimulation.Aim.cs::PrimaryCandidates()` currently yields only enemies and the active Wyrm, while `ArenaEnvironmentState` already supports real physical barrels/crates, projectile impacts, salvage drops and LOS. Right-side tap-to-point expires after **350 ms**, so tapping a crate can fail if a spell is still cooling down.

**Recommended input hierarchy:** **active deliberate manual aim** (existing continuous drag/mouse/right-stick) **→ explicit tap/click on a valid breakable prop** (temporary C#-owned `prop id` focus, not just a fixed aim angle) **→ automatic hostile acquisition** **→ nearby eligible breakable props only if no hostile target is legal** **→ existing no-target behavior**. On clicking/tapping a hostile, retain the current attack/manual semantics rather than implicitly replacing all controls. The lock ends when the object breaks, leaves range/LOS, becomes unavailable, the Hunt changes/pauses, or the player explicitly changes aim. A suggested short inactivity limit is **3 seconds**, subject to combat testing; an active cooldown must not make the tap silently disappear before the next cast. Hostile acquisition always pre-empts *ambient* prop targeting, but not deliberate player focus.

**Touch ergonomics:** a **stationary tap** that hits an actual prop (using a modest accessible hit area at 320/390px) can focus that object; dragging continues to mean the existing movement or manual-aim stick on its established side. The current 55% split must not steal the movement finger. Desktop click-release over a crate may focus; click/hold and dragging elsewhere retain deliberate manual aim. Do not add a mandatory extra aim button or a permanent lock-on that forces movement toward the object. Give the focused crate a restrained, unmistakable ground/outline cue, not a giant marker.

**Authority and fairness:** keep prop identity **typed separately from enemy IDs**, even if integers coincide. C# validates accessibility, radius, line of sight, health and actual spell/projectile impact. A prop must **never** outrank an active dangerous enemy under ordinary auto-aim; excluded scenery and non-destructible loot chests are interaction/proximity objects, not free combat targets. Preserve projectile wall collision, encounter pacing, mobile controls and once-only drop integrity. Add real Hunt 320/390px input tests for motion, enemy proximity, obscured crates, cooldown, prop break/loot and target reset. **This is a recommendation recorded for subsequent implementation, not a claim it has been implemented or finally playtest-approved.**

#### P1b — loot-box, chest and breakable-container handling (26 October 2026)

**Player-facing outcome:** a hunter can reliably open or break an on-screen loot container with one deliberate tap/click while existing automatic Fireball targeting stays useful and dangerous enemies remain the default focus. This **extends P1's already working hybrid controls**; it is **not implemented yet**. Treat "loot box" as a broad player-facing term, not a new `EnemyKind` or a promise of premium/randomized loot.

| Target category | Ordinary automatic behavior | Direct tap/click on the object | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Breakable barrel/crate/destructible loot box** | Target a **visible, reachable, in-range** eligible prop **only if no legal enemy/Wyrm target is available**. Prefer a nearby prop; never steal an existing hostile target. | Lock onto that **specific C# prop ID** for attacks/casts through at least the next valid attack opportunity, even while enemies are nearby, until an invalidation event. | Existing projectile/area combat damages its real health; only confirmed destruction creates the real item/material drop (e.g. Charred Scrap), **once**. |
| **Openable, non-destructible chest/cache** | **Never auto-shoot** or waste a Fireball on it. | Select/interact when the hunter is within a fair interaction radius, or display a clear approach/interact cue; do **not** invent automatic player movement or loot. | Authoritative interaction validates eligibility and grants/opens the contents exactly once; a blocked/locked chest communicates why. |
| **Already dropped items and materials** | Preserve the game's existing proximity collection and pickup rules; no spell aim or target lock. | Optional tap may identify/show the item, but does not bypass distance, collection or save rules. | Collect through the existing authoritative pickup/satchel system. |
| **Decorative or inaccessible scenery** | Never a target. | No lock; optionally a brief non-interactive cue, never a wasted cast. | No combat, no free materials. |

**Input and focus behavior:** short tap/click **hit-tests the actual world object** rather than memorizing a screen position or transient 350 ms aiming angle; allow a small mobile hit-area expansion without selecting an adjacent enemy or prop. Existing left-thumb movement, second-thumb drag/mouse-held aim, right-stick override, cancellation, pause and UI interactions **keep precedence**. A deliberate breakable-prop focus should survive a Fireball cooldown, then clear promptly when the prop breaks, goes out of range/line-of-sight, the player changes manual aim, the scene changes, or a bounded focus timeout expires. A selected enemy uses ordinary combat/manual aim; no tap-to-walk or mandatory extra button.

**Implementation contracts:** expose distinct **typed world target references** (`enemy`/`wyrm` vs `destructible prop` vs `interactable cache`), so coincident numeric IDs never cross-target. C# alone checks state, range/LOS, damage/interaction, item ownership, drop quantity and loot persistence; JS only reports world-coordinate hit-test/selection intent and displays a restrained selection cue. Auto-prop fallback may not add free attacks or reorder combat priorities; avoid introducing a second targeting engine. Maintain collision barriers and natural projectile misses. Build on `RunSimulation.Aim.cs`, `arena-input.js` and `ArenaEnvironmentState`.

**P1b acceptance criteria (actual playable Hunt, not a mocked input-only demonstration):**

1. With a hostile Hound and a crate in range, **normal auto-aim attacks the Hound**. Tapping the crate switches deliberate focus to that crate until its destruction; then enemy auto-aim resumes.
2. With no enemy in legal range, automatic attacks can break a **nearby eligible, line-of-sight** crate; a crate behind a closed gate, outside range or already destroyed cannot be focused or attacked.
3. Tapping during Fireball cooldown **still reaches the next legal cast**. Dragging to aim, holding the mouse, switching controls, blur/cancel, pause/map transition and a new explicit target release the lock as specified.
4. A non-destructible chest opens only by a **real eligible interaction**, never by auto-fire, and delivers contents only once; a dropped item uses the existing proximity pickup.
5. Verify touch hit-testing and safe overlap with movement at **320/390px**, desktop click behavior, deterministic replay, real device input, no duplicate Charred Scrap and no save regressions.

**Dependency:** this P1b targeting/interaction slice is a prerequisite for making **CV2** breakable props naturally usable, **not a prerequisite for CV1 hit reaction art**. No randomized paid loot-box mechanics are proposed.

### Element-driven physical death and debris — FXP1–FXP6 (owner roadmap direction, 26 October 2026)

## 14. Definition of success and Claude kickoff

An ordinary first-time player can start either the optional story or free exploration, see readable Gothic map/environment, learn an initial school, **auto-aim and auto-cast at legal enemies by default with optional fully manual aiming whenever desired**, survive a short coherent Wyrm Hunt, extract genuine material, research a real mutation without destructive RNG, use the unified Nexus to prepare a meaningful build and visibly improve it in a real combat run. They eventually meet equivalent shared endgame eligibility regardless of story choices, while their private spells remain private unless published with consent.

A competitive player can run **Seasonal Expedition** (routes/knowledge/strategy) or **Seasonal Arena** (generated wave survival) under the same frozen four-week rules, retry freely, have the best **independently verified** result placed on distinct boards, and use earned powerful permanent rewards through the Nexus to push further in their **Eternal Hunt**.

An Eternal player can save at a **secured refuge**, quit, relaunch the **town** (not a battle), use **Continue Eternal Hunt**, return to the **same refuge, same world, same committed routes**, suffer recoverable death setbacks and build a lifelong verified frontier without arbitrary seasonal wipe.
