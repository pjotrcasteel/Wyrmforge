## Refuge is gameplay, not an art preview — LOCKED

- The latest main release at this consolidation checkpoint is **0.0.138**. The Refuge shows a coherent **1672×941 night panorama** composed with independent **actual active/vacant artwork** for four buildings. Those states are driven by persistent gameplay unlocks; **no preview/test toggle** may alter them. The town menu opens real destinations; locked taps explain requirements, never silently enter a building.
- **Arcane Nexus** and **Codex** unlock after the first completed Hunt; **Blacksmith** after securing the first Essence; **Hall of Fame** after two completed Hunts. The **World Gate** is always reachable. Use these same authoritative predicates for rendering and navigation. [Town integration evidence](../art/refuge-night-panorama-preview-2026-10-20.md).
- Players navigate the town by **swiping on touch** or dragging on desktop; keyboard access remains for accessibility. **No West/Gate/East buttons** or temporary building toggles. Don't turn approved scenery into vertically stacked image cards.
- Preserve the eight owner-approved active/vacant source PNGs in `user_handover_files/concepts/buildings/`, and their coherent night town composition. Vacant means empty and unpowered (no lights, fire or static NPC); active artwork genuinely lights up. **Never use a CSS dark wash, filter, blackout rectangle or stretch an image** to fake vacancy or fill a screen. Original artwork is immutable without approval. Approved art source details: [canonical building checkpoint](../art/refuge-canonical-buildings-game-screen-2026-10-20.md).
- Day/night adaptation is approved: **Auto** uses local browser/device time, with explicit **Day** and **Night** overrides in Settings; avoid hardcoded server time, location permission or unapproved game-clock pressure. The device-local Auto/Day/Night implementation has merged via [PR #173](https://github.com/pjotrcasteel/Wyrmforge/pull/173). **Day is 07:00–18:59 and Night 19:00–06:59** according to browser/device local time; Settings overrides persist, and town lighting does not change any building unlock.

## Ambient townspeople and painted-world navigation (updated 20 October 2026)

- **Current behaviour:** autonomous pedestrians use seeded C# decisions and a terrain-aware pathfinder. The visual placeholders are deliberately larger until character art is accepted.
- The 1672×941 nighttime town artwork has a matching source map in `wwwroot/art/town/night-navigation.json`: walkable ground polygons, solid footprints, roaming destinations and art-aligned foreground/occlusion regions. Foot coordinates are in **intrinsic painting pixels**, independent of phone viewport, swiping or browser zoom.
- **NPC physics is a small circular feet disc**, not the entire body rectangle or the artwork's height. Scene collision obstacles contain only the portion that contacts the paving (well's stone base, posts at ground level, planted flower beds, building foundations). Elevated roofs/banners are **visual geometry only**; notably the World Gate arch roof is not solid. NPCs may walk in front of a prop with their head overlapping its painted pixels as long as their feet clear the contact footprint.
- The existing original panorama does not provide separate alpha-transparent foreground pieces. Previous all-or-nothing occlusion hid whole rectangular citizens when their body touched a roof or sign, causing unexplained disappearances. **While NPCs are prototype rectangles, keep them fully visible**. Debugging shows the visual occlusion regions in amber, independently of the red ground colliders. When proper art layers exist, use depth ordering and alpha masks to render behind objects accurately—never reintroduce rectangle chopping or sudden full-body hiding.
- **Adding world props:** register a semantic collision polygon using `TownNavigationMap.SetObstacle` (or `RemoveObstacle`) when the prop is placed, moved or removed. A* automatically replans affected NPC routes using the map revision. Do **not** create one-off hardcoded NPC paths around a cart, barrels, a market stall or another temporary obstacle.
- A permanently repainted backdrop also requires updating its corresponding navigation and foreground geometry. Pixel imagery cannot safely infer which details are walkable, solid or depth-occluding; keep the map authored and tested alongside the painting.
- **20 October iPhone screenshot correction:** The first ground map let NPCs traverse banner plinths and garden walls, and the well foreground did not conceal the rectangle above the roof/sign. Restrict the plaza to safe paved corridors, register the central well, both upper-banner bases, both lower lamp pillars and adjacent garden walls as separate solid polygons, and expand the well's foreground clip over the upper silhouette. Give both upper banner bases and both lower lamp pillars their own same-art foreground depth masks, so pedestrians render behind those structures instead of on top of them. Pedestrian destination anchors must remain connected; tests prove that dynamic carts still trigger detours.
- Navigation JSON loads with the application version as a cache key: Safari must not keep the previous colliders while showing a new game build. Approved source artwork and unlock states remain unchanged.
- Authoring acceptance: compare source polygons with the original painting and verify on actual iPhone Safari after deployment. These conservative boundaries intentionally favor staying off props over using every painted cobblestone; exact occlusion/collision positioning still requires live motion acceptance.
- **21 October screenshot regression:** the eastern lower flowerbed extends further onto the apparent paved route than the previous collider. Register an independent `east-lower-planter-inset` footprint, test those points as blocked, preserve all eleven connected town destinations and maintain at least a full rectangle's width of citizen separation. Keep placeholder width/height proportional to the artwork, not viewport `dvh`, so they have the same physical footprint at phone and desktop sizes.
- **21 October town geometry debugger:** The runtime navigation map also includes **named solid polygons for Arcane Nexus, Blacksmith, Codex, Hall of Fame and the World Gate walls/roof**. The roof/walls are impassable but the paved approaches remain available. Game art is still immutable; authored scene geometry is the single reusable collision authority.
- Open the real game with `?townGeometry=1` after the normal URL to enable the read-only developer overlay. **Translucent red = the obstacle's physical footprint at ground level**, **amber = visual occlusion regions**, **cyan = walkable floor**, and **white circles = NPC feet collision radii**. These are the actual runtime simulation shapes. Disable debug by removing the query parameter or visiting the normal URL. Overlays never capture touch input or change unlock flags; no building preview toggle is restored.
- **21 October red-inspector revision:** the earlier polygons followed roof and banner silhouettes, incorrectly marking paving as blocked. Ground-footprint geometry now follows the bases, leaving open paving in front of and behind the well, with a clear gate arch. This first revision is still approximate and needs mobile visual sign-off before pixel-perfect claims.
- The first building polygons are conservative art-matched approximations for visual inspection. **Do not claim exact silhouettes** until the owner validates them on mobile. Existing `SetObstacle`/`RemoveObstacle` update the runtime collision authority and the debug view automatically; future placements can use these same semantic footprints. A future edit mode can support vertex dragging, but this first inspector is intentionally read-only.
- **21 October, exterior roaming and quiet geometry:** NPCs may leave the square via authored west/east lanes, the southern road and the **World Gate arch**. All are connected A* destinations in the same scene map, marked `isExit: true`. Citizens traverse the actual pavement to those points, disappear only after reaching an exit, remain away briefly, then return from the same passage while hidden until safely inside; `LastExitId` and `DepartureCount` leave room for future adventurer/hunt behaviour. There is no instant teleport visible in the plaza, and the system is independent of the player's camera/swipes. Side lanes and the arch remain inspectable in the geometry debugger.
- The debug overlay is deliberately **shapes only**: no large legend and no floating obstacle labels. Red physical footprints now also cover the inner lower-right terrace planter and the foreground garden/parapets. Cyan walkable boundaries and the small white NPC foot discs remain visible in `?townGeometry=1`. Validate exact outlines with owner screenshots; the initial extension is not a claim of perfect geometry.
- **21 October v0.0.148 — NPC scene performance and marked colliders:** iPhone screenshots reveal the well noticeboard base and eastern stair/foreground garden collision gaps. Add `well-noticeboard-post` and `east-stair-railing-planter` footprints, and extend the front-right ground garden without extending collision to elevated artwork. Verify all named town destinations remain walkable and connected.
- Keep the **static approved panorama, vacant layers, building hotspots and debug solid/cyan/amber polygons out of the frequent NPC render loop**. `RefugeTownActors` owns its own 100 ms actor/feet update; `CanonicalTownView` only renders the stable scene at scene/parameter changes, never on the animation timer.
- The C# pathfinder memoizes A* grid walkability and neighbor-edge checks **per navigation revision**; `SetObstacle` and `RemoveObstacle` invalidate those answers. A citizen follows a previously validated path without re-testing its remaining segment every animation tick, and replans on obstacle revision changes. Keep deterministic state, same feet-only collision and dynamic cart avoidance. Tests enforce cached repeated routes and obstacle-revision updates. Assess real phone responsiveness and framerate against the game rather than assuming unit tests prove a performance target.
- **Future work:** workers only for genuinely unlocked buildings; explicit workplace entrance/schedule/action locations, with the same collision/pathfinding rules and suitable foreground cutouts. No worker visuals at vacant locations. NPC movement does not change achievements or player progression.

## First-run and gate journey — LOCKED direction

- Character creation chooses a permanent Divine school and signature, separate from World Gate Hunt/Wyrm lineage. The character-creation copy must be short and meaningful; show recognizable pixel emblems with one accessible label each, not repeated overpainted words.
- The owner has requested **Fire-only character creation temporarily** while completing Fire end-to-end. Render the other five schools disabled without implying they are available. This is implemented for new players on main v0.0.138 through [PR #172](https://github.com/pjotrcasteel/Wyrmforge/pull/172); existing non-Fire characters retain their saved selection.
- The World Gate leads to **Free Play** and a visibly unavailable grayscale Campaign route as appropriate to actually implemented story content. New Hunt is active; **Endless Hunt** and **Endless Arena** are visibly future/disabled options until built. A Free Play new Hunt chooses available lineage and Wyrm tier; never pretend Tier II–V or a missing region is playable. Keep class choice and region choice separate.
- Campaign remains **optional** by product design and is not the exclusive gate to research/endgame. Disabled story UI indicates implementation readiness, not an eventual requirement to finish it for free-play progression.

## UI conventions and accessibility

- Art-led mobile-first composition; strong visual hierarchy with one obvious primary action, progressive disclosure, legible small-screen label, safe-area support and sensible tap targets. Avoid generic filler/AI-sounding narration and repeated instructions.
- HUD/player identity, version and levels are overlays **on top of** authored artwork rather than reserved footer/header bands that reduce the art. Preserve aspect ratio, no stretched town/building illustrations. Browsers may capture gestures: accessible keyboard/focus alternatives must remain.
- The player sees world and spells honestly: a painted bridge is traversable only if the simulation permits it; a disabled icon cannot secretly start a Hunt; color alone isn't the only state signal.
- Inputs must survive cancel/pause/background/resize and page scroll must not hijack combat gestures. Reduce motion and sound when configured.
