Playback and reuse

These are actual renders of the same deforming 112-bone dragon, seen from eight independent cameras. The ground grid follows the authored root-travel curve for foot-contact inspection. Fire and smoke are separate game VFX; enable Attachments to inspect the mouth, claws and wing tips.

Use phase-zero handoffs for locomotion starts/stops. Breath has separate start, loop and end clips; takeoff and landing connect to the flight loop. Death holds its last pose. Future tiers can reuse the skeleton, scale travel/socket offsets, and add bone-bound horns or armor. Recheck framing and deformation after geometry changes.
