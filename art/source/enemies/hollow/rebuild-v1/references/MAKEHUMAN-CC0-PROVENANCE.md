## H1 source attempt and evidence boundaries

`tools/art/hollow-rebuild/sculpt_cc0_anatomy_blender.py` creates a **new editable source-study .blend** under `artifacts/hollow-rebuild/cc0-sculpt-anatomy/` by preserving anatomical topology while applying an isolated asymmetrical, forward-weighted static body-form transformation. It creates real Blender eight-facing clay review images. It has not been cosmetically sculpted to completion, does not author missing anatomical damage, does not supply a rig, and does not modify the generic animation engine.

The workflow `Hollowed isolated visual source gate` runs this as an **independent, unapproved art proof**. Successful execution only confirms a reproducible source and genuine Blender render, not a finished H1 identity, H2 deformation-ready topology, H3 materials, H4 emission, or H5/H6 art acceptance.
